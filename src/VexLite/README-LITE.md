# Vex Lite

A little character in a teal hoodie who lives on your taskbar. He keeps out of the way of whatever you're working on, reminds you to take breaks, drink water and have lunch, and reminds you about anything you ask him to.

## Getting him going

1. Save **Vex Lite.exe** somewhere it can stay, for example your Documents folder. It's a single small file, and there's nothing to install.
2. Double-click it. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* because the file isn't from a big software company: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Answer the few questions (your name, and a couple of choices), then click **Off we go**.

If your work computer won't let you run it, ask your IT team. Some workplaces only allow approved programs.

## Using him

| | |
|---|---|
| **Click him** | Opens his panel: set a reminder, see what's coming up, or tell him you've had a break or gone for lunch |
| **Setting a reminder** | In *when*, type something like `20m`, `1h30m`, `14:30`, `9am`, `tomorrow 9:00` or `fri 16:00`. For a repeating one: `daily 10:00`, `weekdays 14:00` or `mon,thu 9:30`. Then type what it's about and press **Add** |
| **When a reminder goes off** | He knocks on the screen. **OK**, or **10 min** to hear it again later. Repeating ones also have **Stop these** |
| **Ctrl+Alt+V** | Opens his panel from anywhere |
| **Drag him** | Pick him up and put him somewhere else, even on top of a window |
| **Click his mug** | When you refill your own coffee. He keeps count. Right-click the mug to undo |
| **Right-click him** | Setup, Theme (light or dark look, hoodie colour), Quit. Hoodie lightness is in Setup → Theme |

He only nudges you about one thing every 15 minutes or so, and keeps quiet while you're on a call or something is full screen. A nudge you didn't answer waits in the panel's **Pending** tab.

**Going home:** in Setup, put the time you leave and how you get home, and he'll tell you when to pack up, with a map of the way home.

**Meetings:** if you use classic Outlook, he'll remind you about meetings too.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. He notices which program is in front (to suggest a break after a long stretch) and whether your microphone is in use (to stay quiet in calls), but never saves or sends either. He doesn't appear in screenshots or when you share your screen.

## Removing him

Right-click him, **Setup…**, untick *Start me with Windows*, then **Quit**. Delete **Vex Lite.exe**, and the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Vex Lite` if you want his settings gone too.
